Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need previous work experience?

Not necessarily. Some opportunities require professional experience, while others may be suitable for beginners with relevant skills and willingness to learn.

Can I apply from a rural area or work from home?

Yes. We welcome applications from women around the world, including rural and underserved communities. Opportunities will depend on the nature of the work and the requirements of each opportunity.

Can I apply for more than one opportunity?

Yes. You can select multiple areas in the application form.

Are all opportunities paid?

Payment arrangements vary by opportunity. Any salary, project fee, instructor compensation or affiliate commission will be discussed before an engagement begins.

Does submitting an application guarantee work?

No. Applications are reviewed based on suitability and available opportunities. Submission does not guarantee employment, projects or income.

Is there an application fee?

No. HIJABIAN does not charge an application fee to submit this form.

Can women apply from outside Pakistan?

Yes. Women worldwide are welcome to apply. Availability, eligibility and payment arrangements may vary depending on the opportunity and country.
