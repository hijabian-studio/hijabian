Who We Are

More Choices. More Possibilities.

HIJABIAN is a women-focused platform built around a simple belief: every woman deserves the opportunity to use her abilities, develop her potential and create opportunities for herself.

We believe professional ambitions and family responsibilities do not have to compete with one another. Women should have opportunities to learn new skills, share their knowledge, work from home and earn according to their interests, abilities and available time.

Traditional employment is not the right fit for everyone. Fixed schedules, demanding commitments and inflexible working arrangements can make it difficult for many talented women to participate. HIJABIAN aims to offer a more flexible approach.

We bring together learning, teaching, career guidance, referrals, freelance tasks and business opportunities in one growing ecosystem.

Our Approach

Earn According to Your Contribution

We believe in transparent and fair earning arrangements. Different opportunities have different responsibilities and payment structures.

Teaching may have agreed hourly, course-based or monthly compensation. Referrals may provide commission for eligible successful enrolments. Project-based assignments may have fixed payments agreed before work begins.

Every opportunity should explain its responsibilities, time requirements, payment and applicable conditions clearly. Earnings depend on the opportunity, work completed and agreed terms; income is not automatic or guaranteed.
